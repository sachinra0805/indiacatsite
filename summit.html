Flagship event

India Cat Risk Summit

The annual gathering of India's catastrophe modeling profession — practitioners, vendors, (re)insurers, and researchers under one roof. First edition in planning.

Why a summit

Every profession needs its main stage.

India hosts one of the world's largest cat modeling workforces, yet has no flagship industry event of its own. The Summit changes that — a day of deep technical content, honest debate, and the networking that careers are built on.

◈

Technical depth

Model validation, new perils, AI in modeling, open-source engines — content for practitioners, by practitioners. No sales pitches disguised as sessions.

◎

India's perils, front and center

Cyclone, flood, earthquake, and severe convective storm — the perils that shape Indian portfolios get the main stage they deserve.

✦

The room where it happens

CoE leaders, vendor scientists, underwriters, and the next generation — the connections that don't happen over email.
