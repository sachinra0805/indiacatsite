The Association

A profession deserves a professional body.

The Cat Modeling Network Association exists to give India's catastrophe modelers what every mature profession has: standards, mentorship, and a collective voice.

Our mission

Raise the bar for cat modeling — in India and beyond.

Catastrophe modeling sits at the intersection of atmospheric science, engineering, statistics, and finance. In India, thousands of practitioners do this work every day for the world's largest (re)insurers — often without a shared forum to learn, debate, and grow together.

The Association exists to close that gap: vendor-neutral education, practitioner-led community, and a credible voice for Indian modeling talent in global forums.

  • Vendor-neutral technical education and model literacy
  • Mentorship from analysts to center-of-excellence leaders
  • Representation of Indian talent in global industry bodies
  • Ethical, rigorous standards for model use and communication
