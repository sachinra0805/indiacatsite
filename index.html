About the association
Why IndiaCat exists

The world's risk is modeled in India. The community should be too.

India is one of the largest catastrophe modeling talent hubs on the planet. Global insurers, reinsurers, brokers and model vendors run centers of excellence here — yet the community has never had a professional home of its own.

IndiaCat changes that: a place to learn rigorously, connect genuinely, and raise the bar for the profession — from analyst to center head.

  • Vendor-neutral — RMS, AIR, Verisk and open-source alike
  • Practitioner-led — built by modelers, for modelers
  • Career-long — from first principles to leadership
