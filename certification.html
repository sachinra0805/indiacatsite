Certification

A credential built for how India models risk.

The IndiaCat certification is India's own catastrophe risk designation — rigorous, practical, and recognized by the employers who hire here. Currently in development with founding members.

Why a new credential

Global designations weren't built for this market.

Existing catastrophe risk credentials are designed around US and European market practice. India's modeling industry — CoE workflows, vendor platforms, portfolio structures, and perils — deserves a credential that tests what Indian modelers actually do.

◈

Practice-first

Assessed on real modeling work — exposure cleansing, model validation, results interpretation — not just theory.

◎

Vendor-neutral

Tests the four modules and professional judgment, not loyalty to any single platform.

✦

Employer-recognized

Designed with hiring CoE leaders so the letters actually move careers and compensation.

The levels

Three levels, one standard: rigor.

A career-long ladder — from first principles to professional leadership.

Level 1

IndiaCat Certified Associate (ICA)

Foundations of catastrophe modeling: the four modules, key metrics, and reading model output with understanding.

  • Four-module examination
  • Metrics & interpretation
  • Target: 0–2 years experience
Level 2

IndiaCat Certified Professional (ICP)

Production fluency: vendor platforms, model validation, exposure management, and communicating loss to underwriters.

  • Platform practicum
  • Validation case study
  • Target: 2–5 years experience
Level 3

Fellow, IndiaCat (FIC)

Leadership in the profession: model governance, CoE strategy, mentorship, and contribution to the body of knowledge.

  • Portfolio & peer review
  • Professional contribution
  • Target: 5+ years / leadership
How it will work

Built in the open, with practitioners.

The curriculum, assessments, and standards are being developed with founding members — working modelers, validators, and CoE leaders — not handed down from a committee room.

  • Curriculum mapped to the IndiaCat learning paths
  • Examinations plus practical assessment
  • Continuing professional development to maintain standing
  • Grandfathering pathway for experienced practitioners

Pilot assessments targeted for 2027. Founding members shape the standard.
