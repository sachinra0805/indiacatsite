FAQ

Questions new modelers ask

Do I need an atmospheric science degree to become a cat modeler?

No. Most Indian modelers come from engineering, statistics, mathematics, or finance backgrounds. The peril science can be learned on the job; what matters is quantitative rigor, curiosity about physical processes, and comfort with data at scale.

Which vendor platform should I learn first?

Learn the four modules first — they're platform-agnostic. Then pick whichever platform your team or target employer uses; Touchstone and RMS cover the majority of Indian CoE roles. The concepts transfer.

How is cat modeling different from data science?

Cat modeling is domain-first: the physics and engineering of perils constrain everything. A pure ML approach without peril science produces elegant nonsense. The best modelers combine statistical skill with genuine understanding of hazard and vulnerability.

Is Python really used in production cat modeling?

Increasingly, yes — for pre/post-processing, validation, custom analytics, and now GPU-accelerated engines. Vendor UIs still dominate execution, but Python is where analysts differentiate themselves.

What does career growth look like?

Analyst → senior analyst → model validator / team lead → CoE leadership, or lateral moves into underwriting, capital modeling, and climate risk. See our careers page for the full map.
